## P2 — Bloque 3: Hardening (MEDIUM/LOW) — spec `ci-workflow-hardening` + mods de `ci-secret-scanning` / `ci-scheduled-security-review`

- [ ] 3.1 Permissions least-privilege: `ci.yml` (líneas 3-6) → workflow-level `contents: read` SOLO (quitar `pull-requests: write` y `checks: write` a nivel workflow); los 5 jobs que corren `dorny/test-reporter` (test-unit-client, test-unit-server, test-integration, test-smoke, e2e) reciben job-level `checks: write` + `contents: read`; jobs `changes`, `build`, `zombie-workflow-guard` se quedan con el default (`contents: read`). `security.yml` job `sbom` → `permissions: contents: read` (quitar `actions: write`). NO reducir `release.yml` (`contents: write` + `pull-requests: write` requeridos por changesets) ni `preview.yml` (`pull-requests: write` requerido por comentarios de PR). **Spec**: "Least-privilege workflow and job permissions" (2 escenarios)
- [ ] 3.2 `timeout-minutes` en jobs sin él (design D6): `ci.yml` job `changes` 5; `quality.yml` job `quality` 15; `release.yml` job `release` 10; `scheduled-security.yml` job `gitleaks-full-scan` 15; `security-digest.yml` jobs sbom 10 / vulnerability-review 10 / digest 10; `security.yml` jobs dependency-scan 15 / sast 15 / secrets 10 / sbom 10 / dependency-review 10. **Spec**: "Bounded job runtimes"

- [ ] 3.4 SHA pinning full-length (40 chars) con comentario `# vX.y.z` en TODAS las actions third-party de los 8 workflows **INCLUYENDO el namespace `actions/*`** (checkout, setup-node, cache, github-script, upload/download-artifact — Scorecard recomienda pinarlo todo, no solo third-party ajenas); incluye `codeql-action/init|analyze|upload-sarif`; el composite local queda path-referenced (`.github/actions/setup-monorepo`). **Refs `docker://`** (gitleaks ×3: scheduled-security.yml:24,36 y security.yml:77) y **service images** (postgres/floci en ci.yml/preview.yml): NO admiten SHA de 40 chars de action — pinar por **digest** (`docker://image@sha256:...`) o documentar exclusión explícita.
